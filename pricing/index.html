Billing questions

Why is there an annual plan for a flower that blooms once a decade?

Because the monthly plan churned. People would sign up, wait three months, decide nothing was happening and cancel. They were right, but she bloomed the week after.

What happens if she blooms twice in one billing year?

It's never happened. If it does, we will issue a press release before we issue an invoice.

Do you offer refunds?

No. We've issued zero refunds in the life of the company, which is mostly because the life of the company has had one bloom in it.

Do you have a nonprofit discount?

Nearly every customer is a nonprofit, so the discount is the price. Dale isn't a nonprofit. He pays full price and he's happy about it.
