Customers

People who were there

What if she doesn't bloom?

Your booking rolls over to the next bloom. If that's after you die, it goes to whoever you named in your will. The booking form has a clause for this, which you agreed to.

Do you support other plants?

No. Booking software for everyone is the most crowded market in software. Booking software for one specific flower in one specific glasshouse has no competitors at all. We checked. The 17-year cicada came up, and we decided it's a different vertical.

Why does she smell like that?

When Barb blooms she heats her spadix to about body temperature and pushes out dimethyl trisulfide, which smells like rotting meat, and isovaleric acid, which smells like feet. It brings in carrion beetles. It's working as designed.

Can I pick my time slot?

No. Barb opens when she opens, usually at night. You get 15 minutes, and the time is in the text.

Is there a mobile app?

There's a push notification. It fires once a decade at 3am. That's the app.

What data do you keep?

Your name, your phone number and your stench tolerance. The tolerance is the sensitive one. Nothing on this page is actually sent anywhere, because StinkSlot isn't real.
